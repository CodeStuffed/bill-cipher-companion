# Bill 1.0.0 — The Dream Demon Comes to macOS

Bill Cipher lives on your desktop: a hand-drawn 380-sprite pixel-art companion, native Swift end to end (AppKit + SpriteKit + WebKit), who roams your screen, studies your apps, watches the sky, and chats through his own speech bubbles.

> Unofficial, non-commercial fan project. Bill Cipher and Gravity Falls are © Disney. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Disney.

## Install

1. Download **Bill-macOS.dmg** below (a plain `Bill-macOS.zip` is there too).
2. Open it and drag **Bill** onto the **Applications** shortcut beside him.
3. First launch: **right-click → Open** (ad-hoc signed, not notarized).
4. Check the menu bar. He's already watching — and shortly he'll offer to study your apps.

**Requires** macOS 26 (Tahoe) or newer, and a ChatGPT account signed in once inside Bill's own chat window (he has his own browser; your normal login doesn't carry over).

## Highlights

- **First-run personalization** — Bill uses his embedded ChatGPT session to write himself a morning / midday / afternoon / night line for the apps actually on your Mac, plus transition quips for your most-used pairs. Generated on your machine, in his voice, referencing real Gravity Falls lore — and live the moment each batch lands.
- **Weather that keeps up** — keyless Open-Meteo radar nowcast refreshed every 5 minutes: he announces rain while it's *starting* to rain, and today's conditions ride into his chat context.
- **Pixel chat, fixed properly** — conversations scroll instead of clipping, the panel can never end up off-screen, `Shift+Return` writes paragraphs, clicking the box focuses, `Esc` always closes, and the newest message is always in view.
- **Anchored blasts** — shooting-style animations no longer shove Bill backward; his body stays planted while the beams fly.
- **No more invisible border** — ambient bark bubbles wrap to the on-screen part of his window, so a line can never be silently cut off again.
- **Canon-rich dialogue** — turn the volume up for his thoughts on the party at the Shack, drain the battery for Time Baby's opinion, and lose the network to hear about the mindscape being down for maintenance.
